Health Screening and Promotion Center

A dedicated screening facility, with views of the Hangang River.

Opened in 1990, with programs tailored by gender, age and disease. Depending on your schedule, a variety of comprehensive examinations can be done in a single day.

  • 8,300 m² dedicated facility with views of the Hangang River

  • When needed, results connect to AMC's specialized centres for treatment or referral

International patient support

Since 1989, a department for patients from abroad.

AMC's International Healthcare Center supports international patients in four stages, from before the visit to follow-up at home.

  1. 01

    Pre-visit

    • Reviewing medical reports
    • Treatment plan and cost estimation
    • Lodging and transportation arrangement
  2. 02

    Treatment

    Appointments and examinations, with concierge coordinators in the hospital.

  3. 03

    Discharge

    Admission and discharge, billing, medical reports and CDs, handled in one place.

  4. 04

    Follow-up

    Follow-up care by call or email.

Questions

Good to know before you write.

Is Camnemi the hospital?
No. Camnemi is the Official Agency of Asan Medical Center. We coordinate your inquiry and explain each step. Clinical services, medical decisions, appointments and billing are handled by Asan Medical Center.
Can Camnemi tell me which treatment is right for me?
No. Camnemi and this website do not give medical advice, diagnose or recommend treatment. Clinical decisions are made by the treating medical professionals at AMC.
Where do I send my medical records?
Not in chat and not in this form. If records are needed, our medical team will give you Camnemi's secure registration page and tell you exactly what to prepare.
Which language can I use?
You can write to Camnemi in English or Khmer. Interpretation inside the hospital is provided by AMC's international services; Khmer support is provided by Camnemi's own coordinators.
Why Asan Medical Center?
Asan Medical Center is in Seoul, Korea. In Newsweek and Statista's World's Best Specialized Hospitals 2027, AMC is ranked in the global top 10 in five specialties: endocrinology (#2), gastroenterology (#5), urology (#5), oncology (#6) and neurology (#9). These are specialty rankings, not an overall hospital ranking, and they do not predict the outcome for any individual patient. Whether a service is possible for you is decided by AMC after review.
Is a video consultation the same as a second opinion?
No. A video consultation is a real-time session with a local physician present. A second opinion is a written opinion from AMC specialists based on submitted records. Whether either is possible is decided by AMC after review.
