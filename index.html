Inaugurated on June 23, 1989, Asan Medical Center stands as the largest tertiary hospital in Korea. It was established to embody the founding principle of founder Chung Ju-yung: “Helping the underprivileged of our society.”

World-class procedures at Asan Medical Center

Facial Contouring

V-line · Zygoma Reduction · Genioplasty

Asan's facial bone surgery team is recognized globally for precision V-line surgery and 3D-guided facial contouring. Over 300 complex facial bone cases handled annually with custom surgical planning.

Double Jaw Surgery

Orthognathic · TMJ Correction · Sleep Apnea

Asan's oral and maxillofacial plastic surgery team performs complex orthognathic procedures combining functional correction with aesthetic harmony, using virtual surgical planning for predictable outcomes.

Rhinoplasty

Primary · Revision · Ethnic Asian Rhinoplasty

Specializing in Asian rhinoplasty with autologous tissue techniques. Asan surgeons are internationally invited lecturers on complex revision rhinoplasty — a testament to their mastery in nasal reconstruction.

Microsurgical Reconstruction

Free Flap · Nerve Repair · Lymphatic Surgery

Asan Medical Center's microsurgery program is world-class — with a 98.7% success rate. Surgeons from over 30 countries have completed fellowships here, including MD Anderson Cancer Center alumni returning to learn advanced techniques.

Blepharoplasty

Double Eyelid · Ptosis Correction · Canthoplasty

Home to world leaders in Asian blepharoplasty. The department's oculoplastic specialists handle over 1,000 eyelid procedures annually, from subtle double-eyelid creation to complex revision ptosis repair.

Anti-Aging &amp; Lifting

Facelift · Fat Grafting · Thread Lifting

Combining surgical precision with aesthetic artistry, Asan's anti-aging team offers deep-plane facelifts, structural fat grafting, and minimally invasive lifting — all backed by hospital-grade safety protocols private clinics cannot match.

Breast Aesthetic Surgery

Augmentation · Mastopexy · Reduction · Reconstruction

Asan's breast surgery specialists provide the full spectrum of aesthetic and reconstructive breast procedures — from augmentation mammoplasty and mastopexy to reduction and microsurgical reconstruction — delivering natural-looking results with oncologic safety.
