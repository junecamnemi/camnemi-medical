What AMC offers

Three ways to begin.

Video consultation, second opinion and health screening are different services with different requirements. Whether any of them is possible is decided by AMC after review.

Provided by Asan Medical Center

Video consultation

A real-time video consultation between you, your local physician and an AMC physician.

  • AMC case coordinators and interpreters support the session
  • A full written report follows the consultation
Requirement: a local physician must be present during the consultation.

Provided by Asan Medical Center

Second opinion

A written opinion from AMC specialists, based on the medical records you submit.

  • Written only — there is no video element
  • Records go through Camnemi's secure registration page, never through chat
This is a different service from video consultation.

Health screening

The Health Screening and Promotion Center offers programs tailored by gender, age and disease.

  • Depending on the schedule, a variety of comprehensive examinations can be done in a single day
  • Appointment scheduling, guidance and interpretation are provided for overseas patients
Program options and scheduling are confirmed by Asan Medical Center.

Questions

Good to know before you write.

Is Camnemi the hospital?
No. Camnemi is the Official Agency of Asan Medical Center. We coordinate your inquiry and explain each step. Clinical services, medical decisions, appointments and billing are handled by Asan Medical Center.
Can Camnemi tell me which treatment is right for me?
No. Camnemi and this website do not give medical advice, diagnose or recommend treatment. Clinical decisions are made by the treating medical professionals at AMC.
Where do I send my medical records?
Not in chat and not in this form. If records are needed, our medical team will give you Camnemi's secure registration page and tell you exactly what to prepare.
Which language can I use?
You can write to Camnemi in English or Khmer. Interpretation inside the hospital is provided by AMC's international services; Khmer support is provided by Camnemi's own coordinators.
Is a video consultation the same as a second opinion?
No. A video consultation is a real-time session with a local physician present. A second opinion is a written opinion from AMC specialists based on submitted records. Whether either is possible is decided by AMC after review.
